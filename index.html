Privacy Policy

Bubimo: Diary and Journal

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Applies to version 1.2.4 and later · This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Bubimo.

In short

  • Your diary entries are stored only on your device — we never see them.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share your personal diary data with anyone.
  • Signing in is optional, and only needed if you choose to back up to your own Google Drive.
  • Internet is used to load sticker/background images and to show at most one interstitial ad per day.
  • Prefer no ads? Simply turn off your internet connection before using the app.
  • All diary and journaling features are completely free.
01

Overview

Bubimo ("the app", "we", "us") is a diary and journaling app developed and operated from Kerala, India. This policy describes what information the app handles, how it is stored, and the choices you have. By using Bubimo, you agree to the practices described here.

02

Information we do not collect

Bubimo does not collect, transmit, or share any of the following with us or any third party:

  • Your diary or journal entries, titles, or attached notes
  • Photos or images you add to entries
  • Your app-lock PIN, pattern, or security question answers

Bubimo does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Crashlytics. The app displays at most one interstitial (full-screen) advertisement per day, served through Google AdMob — see Section 7 for details on how this works and what data it involves.

03

How your data is stored

All diary content is stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database. This data never leaves your phone unless you explicitly choose to back it up (see Section 5).

Important: if you uninstall the app without first creating a backup, all locally stored diary entries, images, and settings will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us. We do not hold a copy on any server.

04

App Lock and security features

Bubimo offers an optional App Lock feature for extra privacy, using either a PIN or a security question.

  • Your PIN and your security question's answer are stored only in the local database on your device.
  • Neither is transmitted to us or to any external server.
  • We cannot see, recover, or reset your PIN or security answer on your behalf, since we never have access to it.
05

Optional Google sign-in and Drive backup

Signing in is entirely optional and is only required if you want to back up your diary data. If you choose to sign in with Google:

  • Your backup file is uploaded directly to your own Google Drive account — not to any server owned or operated by us.
  • We do not have access to, and cannot view, your backup file or its contents.
  • Sign-in is handled through Google's standard authentication; we only receive the minimum account information (such as your Google account identifier) needed to connect to your Drive for backup and restore.
  • You can revoke this access at any time from your Google Account permissions settings.

If you never use the backup feature, no account information is collected at all.

06

Internet usage

Bubimo uses an internet connection for two purposes: to fetch sticker packs and background presets that you can use to decorate your diary entries, and to load the interstitial advertisement described in Section 7. No diary content, images you've written, or personal information is sent over the internet as part of either process.

The core diary and journaling features work offline. If you are offline, stickers and backgrounds that have not yet been downloaded will not load, and no advertisement will be shown.

07

Advertising

Bubimo shows an interstitial advertisement (a full-screen ad), served through Google AdMob. The app shows at most one such ad per day. The ad can be dismissed using its close button once it appears.

Don't want to see ads? Ads are loaded over the internet, so if you turn off your internet connection (Wi-Fi and mobile data) before using the app, no ad will be shown.

To serve ads, AdMob (via Google) may collect or process information such as your device's advertising identifier, general device information, approximate location (derived from IP address), and ad interaction data (e.g. impressions and clicks). This processing is carried out by Google, not by us — we do not receive or store this data ourselves.

  • We do not access, view, or store the data AdMob collects for ad delivery.
  • This ad data is entirely separate from your diary content, which stays on your device as described in Section 2 and 3.
  • You can manage ad personalization for your device through your Google Account's Ad Settings.
  • For details on how Google's advertising products handle data, see Google's Partner Sites policy and the AdMob data disclosure.
08

Data sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your diary content with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. The third-party services involved are Google Drive (only if you opt in to backups, in which case the data goes directly to your own Drive storage) and Google AdMob (for serving the daily interstitial ad, as described in Section 7).

09

10

Children's privacy

Bubimo is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and since diary data never leaves the device (except via an optional, user-initiated Drive backup), no personal diary information is transmitted to us regardless of the user's age.

11

Your rights and choices

  • You can use Bubimo fully without ever creating an account.
  • You control whether backups are created and where they are stored (your own Google Drive).
  • You will see no more than one interstitial ad per day, and you can avoid ads entirely by turning off your internet connection before using the app.
  • You can delete all app data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Since we do not hold your diary data on any server, there is no server-side account or data deletion request needed for that data — deleting it from your device (and your Drive, if backed up) is complete removal.
12

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example to reflect new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of Bubimo after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
